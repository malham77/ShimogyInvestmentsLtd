About SHIMOGY Investment Group Ltd

Built in Kawempe Tula, Kampala to deliver structured enterprise investments, accessible savings, and accountable community credit.

Our Story

Partners for Life Since 2023

SHIMOGY Investment Group Ltd is a Kampala-based investment and community savings organization operating from Kawempe Tula. Founded in 2023, we structure local business ventures, savings accounts, rotating community cycles, and member financing with transparent reporting.

Operating Principles

Our Core Values

01

Transparency

All member transactions, investment slots, savings deposits, and loan approvals are logged and visible in your Client Portal.

02

Accountability

Our Kawempe Tula office maintains verifiable physical business records and in-person client consultation hours.

03

Data Minimization

We collect only the personal details strictly necessary to verify identity, process service requests, and manage member ledgers.

04

Youth Safeguards

Accounts for individuals under 18 require verified parent or legal guardian consent; loans are restricted exclusively to adults 18+.
