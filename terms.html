2. Eligibility & Youth Participation

  • Independent applicants for investments, savings cycles, and client loans must be at least 18 years of age.
  • Savings accounts opened for minors under 18 require documented parent or legal guardian consent, verification, and ongoing supervision.
  • Online request submissions create a preliminary application record; active participation begins only after physical or direct verification by our Operations Team.

3. Investment Risk Disclosure

Enterprise investment plans reflect projected capital commitments and estimated payouts based on active operational cycles in sectors such as poultry, hospitality, retail, transport, and construction. Every business venture involves operational and market risk, and projected returns are not unconditionally guaranteed against unforeseen commercial events.

4. Savings Accounts & Fixed Deposits

  • Current Accounts provide flexible deposits and withdrawals subject to verification during working hours (Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM EAT).
  • Fixed Accounts lock committed savings for the agreed fixed duration at 7% annual interest with zero monthly maintenance fees and up to 85% fixed equivalency loan eligibility.

5. Savings Cycles & Client Loans

Cycle participants agree to uphold scheduled Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or agreed custom contribution commitments. Client loans ranging from UGX 50,000 to UGX 2,000,000 across 15 to 150 day terms are subject to active client standing and timely repayment in accordance with signed loan schedules.
