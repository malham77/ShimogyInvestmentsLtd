Requirements & Transparency

Loan Eligibility & Review Criteria

Loans are issued exclusively to verified members under transparent repayment terms.

01

Active Membership Standing

Applicants must hold an active Current Account, Fixed Account, or active Savings Cycle record with SHIMOGY.

02

18+ Legal Eligibility

Loan facilities are strictly available to adults aged 18 years or older with valid identification verification.

03

85% Fixed Equivalency

Fixed Account holders qualify for priority credit approval up to 85% of their locked deposit balance.

04

Portal Loan Tracking

Once approved by our Operations Team, every loan amount, duration, and status appears live in your Client Portal.
