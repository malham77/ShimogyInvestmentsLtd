Refund & Withdrawal Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Clear rules for deposits, early cancellations, and cycle settlements.

1. Current Savings Account Withdrawals

Current Account holders may withdraw available funds up to their cleared balance at any time during official working hours (Monday - Saturday, 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM EAT) after verification by our Operations Team. There are no monthly account fees.

2. Fixed Deposit Account Early Release

Fixed Accounts earn 7% annual interest when held until the agreed maturity date. If a client requires emergency liquidity before maturity, they are encouraged to utilize an 85% fixed equivalency loan instead of breaking the fixed term. Early liquidation before maturity forfeits accrued fixed interest unless waived in writing for documented medical emergencies.

3. Enterprise Investment Plans

Once an investment slot request is verified and capital is deployed into an active business cycle (30 to 120 days), principal and operational earnings are settled upon completion of the active cycle. Duplicate or erroneous transfers made prior to slot activation are refunded 100% within 48 business hours.

4. Rotating Savings Cycles

Because group cycle contributions fund scheduled client payouts, deposits already disbursed to a cycle recipient cannot be reversed mid-round. Clients wishing to exit a cycle may complete the current rotation or arrange an Operations-approved substitute client.

5. How to Request a Refund or Adjustment

Submit erroneous transfer or withdrawal inquiries at our Kawempe Tula office or via email at shimogygroup@gmail.com with your registered phone number and transaction reference.
