Our Operational Approach

Why Invest With SHIMOGY?

Five principles guiding how we evaluate ventures, allocate slots, and report progress to clients.

01

Local Enterprise Focus

Capital is directed toward tangible Ugandan enterprises in hospitality, retail, agriculture, transport, and construction.

02

Defined Cycle Terms

Every plan states its entry capital, cycle length, risk category, and projected payout upfront before onboarding.

03

Controlled Slot Capacity

Each enterprise accepts a capped number of participant slots so ventures remain properly capitalized and managed.

04

Client Portal Visibility

Approved client investments reflect directly in the Client Portal with status tracking from activation to completion.

05

Accessible Minimums

Plans start from UGX 200,000, enabling first-time investors and growing savers to participate without high barriers.
