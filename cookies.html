Cookie Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Minimal, transparent browser storage practices.

1. What We Store

SHIMOGY Investment Group Ltd avoids invasive cross-site tracking or third-party advertising cookies. We only store minimal essential tokens in your browser:

  • shimogy_cookie_consent: Stores whether you chose "Essential Only" or "Accept All" so the cookie banner does not interrupt repeat visits.
  • shimogy_theme: Remembers your Light or Dark display preference across pages.
  • shimogy_member_token: Secure session token used strictly when you sign into the Client Portal.
  • shimogy_admin_token: Secure session token used strictly when an authorized Operations Administrator signs into the Operations Admin Console.

2. Managing or Resetting Cookie Preferences

You can clear your saved consent choice at any time using the button below or via your browser settings.
