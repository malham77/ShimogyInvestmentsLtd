SHIMOGY Savings Accounts

Flexible day-to-day liquidity with a Current Account, or higher annual returns on a dedicated Fixed Account.

Account Options

Choose Your Savings Account

Both account types can be monitored through your Client Portal with full deposit history.

Flexible Access

Current Account

Everyday deposits and withdrawals with transparent record-keeping

  • Deposit any amount from UGX 5,000 upward
  • Withdraw funds easily with Operations Team verification
  • Suitable for personal goals, retail float, and emergencies
  • Real-time balance visibility in your Client Portal
Interest Bearing

Fixed Deposit

Lock your savings for better returns

  • Earn 7% annual interest on your fixed deposit.
  • Eligible for loan of 85% fixed equivalency
  • No monthly fees charged
  • Guaranteed interest at end of fixed duration.
Youth & Minor Savings Notice: Account holders must be 18 or older. Accounts opened for minors require verified parent or legal guardian consent and supervision.
Onboarding Steps

How to Open Your Account

Open a Current Account or Fixed Account in four clear steps.

01

Select Account Type

Choose Current Account for regular liquidity or Fixed Account for 7% annual interest and loan equivalency.

02

Submit Account Request

Provide your name, contact phone number, and confirm age or guardian supervision requirements.

03

Verify With Operations Team

Our Kawempe Tula team confirms your initial deposit and sets up your Client Portal credentials.

04

Track Your Balance

Sign in anytime via the Client Portal to review deposits, Fixed Account status, and available credit eligibility.
